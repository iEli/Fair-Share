About Us

We are five college kids looking to create a helpful and useful tool for people living with roommates. Our goal is to help people with tracking shared expenses so the everybody can pay their FairShare.

How we got Started

This project started as an idea for CSCI-4440 Software Design and Documentation at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. Tasked with creating a semester long project, we got to work!
